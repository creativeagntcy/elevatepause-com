Dr. Jessica Shepherd

Board-Certified OB-GYN & Menopause Expert  |  Author · Media Contributor · Women's Health Advocate

Menopause Medicine Hormonal Health Women's Longevity Perimenopause

Dr. Jessica Shepherd is a board-certified OB-GYN and leading voice in women's midlife health. Nationally recognized for her work in menopause medicine and hormonal health, she brings evidence-based education and compassionate advocacy to women navigating perimenopause, menopause, and healthy aging. A frequent contributor to national media and author focused on women's longevity, Dr. Shepherd is committed to closing the information gap that leaves too many women without the care and answers they deserve.
