Questions

What is elevatePAUSE?

elevatePAUSE is a clinician-led midlife and longevity summit built by shiftHER. It brings together physicians, menopause specialists, and wellness advocates for two days of evidence-based education, expert guidance, and real conversations about perimenopause, menopause, hormonal health, and healthy aging.

When and where is it happening?

elevatePAUSE 2026 takes place October 2 & 3, 2026 in Atlanta, GA. Exact venue details will be shared with waitlist members first. Seats are limited.

Who should attend?

Women in their 40s and 50s navigating perimenopause or menopause who want evidence-based information, practical tools, and a community that understands what they're going through — without the noise.

What will I walk away with?

A clearer understanding of your hormonal health, practical frameworks for making informed wellness decisions, and the language to advocate for yourself in medical settings. Plus connections with clinicians and a community of midlife women.
