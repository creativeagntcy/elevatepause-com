Questions

What is elevatePAUSE™?

elevatePAUSE™ is a clinician-led midlife and longevity summit powered by shiftHER™ in partnership with Modern Meno. It brings together physicians, menopause specialists, advocates, and innovators for two days of evidence-based education, expert guidance, and real conversation about perimenopause, menopause, hormonal health, and healthy aging. But it is also so much more than that. elevatePAUSE™ is a room full of women who finally feel seen — learning, laughing, connecting, and leaving with more clarity, more confidence, and more joy than they walked in with.

When and where is it happening?

elevatePAUSE™ 2026 takes place October 2 & 3, 2026 in Atlanta, GA. The event spans two days: a VIP Welcome Experience on Friday, October 2 (exclusively for VIP ticket holders) followed by the full-day summit on Saturday, October 3, open to all ticket holders. Exact venue details will be shared with waitlist members first. Seats are limited.

Is this a medical event or a wellness event?

It is both — and the distinction matters. elevatePAUSE™ is clinician-led and evidence-based. You will hear directly from board-certified physicians, menopause specialists, and healthcare advocates. You will also leave with practical tools, lifestyle strategies, and a community that supports your whole health. And yes — there will be joy. A lot of it. This is not a supplement pitch or a somber medical conference. It is the credible, solutions-oriented, and genuinely celebratory conversation midlife women deserve.

Who should attend elevatePAUSE™?

elevatePAUSE™ is for women navigating perimenopause, menopause, or surgical menopause at any age who want evidence-based information, practical tools, and a community that truly gets it. If you have Googled your symptoms at 2am, been dismissed by a provider, or simply want to understand what is happening in your body and what your options are, this summit was built for you. Come ready to learn, connect, and have a genuinely good time doing it.

I had a hysterectomy. Can I come to this event?

Absolutely. elevatePAUSE™ was built with you in mind. Women who have experienced surgical menopause, whether from hysterectomy, oophorectomy, or other procedures, often face an abrupt transition with little preparation or support. You belong in this room. The programming includes content specifically relevant to surgical menopause, hormonal health, and the unique challenges of an early or sudden transition. And the community in that room? They will understand your story in a way most people in your life simply cannot.

I’ve had breast cancer. Is this event for me?

Yes — and you are not alone. Many women navigating menopause after breast cancer feel left out of the conversation because so much of the standard guidance does not apply to them. elevatePAUSE™ is built around the full spectrum of women’s midlife health experiences, including those whose menopause journey was shaped by cancer treatment. You will find clinicians in this room who understand your specific challenges, and a community of women who will not ask you to fit a mold that was never made for you. Come as you are.

What kinds of experts and topics will be covered?

elevatePAUSE™ brings together a multidisciplinary faculty because midlife health requires more than one lens. You will hear from OB-GYNs, cardiologists, functional medicine practitioners, integrative health specialists, mental health experts, nutritionists, fitness specialists, and more — all focused on the intersection of midlife, menopause, and longevity. Featured speaker and faculty member Dr. Jessica Shepherd — board-certified OB-GYN and nationally recognized menopause expert — leads a roster of clinicians and practitioners committed to evidence-based, whole-body care. Full speaker announcements will be shared with the waitlist first.

What will I walk away with?

Answers. Clarity. Community. And honestly — a whole lot of energy. You will leave with a clearer understanding of your hormonal health, practical frameworks for making informed wellness decisions, and the language to advocate for yourself in medical settings. More than that, you will leave having spent two days in a room that reminded you how powerful this season of life actually is. The connections you make, the conversations you have, and the permission you give yourself to prioritize your health — that is what elevatePAUSE™ is really about.
