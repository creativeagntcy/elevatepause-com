Questions

What is elevatePAUSE™?

elevatePAUSE™ is a curated in-person experience for women in midlife, built by shiftHER™. It brings together expert speakers, actionable frameworks, and a community of women navigating perimenopause and menopause — focused on informed decision-making and self-advocacy in healthcare.

When and where is it happening?

elevatePAUSE™ is coming to Atlanta, GA in 2026. Exact date and venue will be announced to the waitlist first. Seats are limited.

Who is this experience designed for?

Women in their 40s and 50s navigating perimenopause or menopause who want evidence-based information, practical tools, and a community that understands what they're going through — without the noise.

What will I actually walk away with?

A clearer understanding of your own body, practical frameworks for making informed wellness decisions, and the language to advocate for yourself in medical settings. Not a supplement stack. Not generic advice.
